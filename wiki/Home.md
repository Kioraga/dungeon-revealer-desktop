Dungeon Revealer Desktop is a local desktop app for tabletop gaming that lets the game master reveal areas of the game map to players on a projector or external screen.

The app opens a **DM window** and, when you click **Start Sharing**, a **fullscreen player window** on the screen of your choice (see [Install](Install.md)). Everything runs locally; there is no server to host and no players connect over the network.

## What the DM Sees

The DM window is where you load maps, draw and erase fog of war, place and move tokens, roll dice and keep notes.

## What the players see

The player window shows the active map and fog of war live, fullscreen, view-only. See [Getting Started](Getting-Started.md) for the full workflow.
